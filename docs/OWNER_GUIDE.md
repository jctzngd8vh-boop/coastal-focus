# Owner Guide

## Getting started

1. Sign in at `/login`. First time ever? You'll see "Create Owner Account" —
   that becomes your store's owner login.
2. Finish the setup wizard: business name, logo, phone/text/email, pickup
   address & hours, tax rate, policies, and which notifications you want.
   You can change any of it later in **Settings**.
3. Add your products in **Products** — photo, description, price, sizes or
   flavors as "option groups," allergen info, and whether to track
   inventory.
4. Add at least one **Payment Method** and one **Fulfillment Method** in
   **Settings** — checkout won't work without both.
5. Save the app to your iPhone home screen for a full-screen, app-like
   experience: Share button → **Add to Home Screen**.

## Taking an order

- **Customers order themselves** at your storefront URL — no account
  needed. They land on a confirmation page with a private link to check
  their order status anytime.
- **You take an order by phone, text, DM, or in person** using **POS**
  (bottom nav). Search for the customer or type their info, tap products to
  add them, pick fulfillment and payment, and save — designed to take under
  a couple of minutes. After saving, use the message composer to text or
  email a confirmation with one tap.

## Managing orders

Every order shows two independent statuses:

- **Fulfillment stage**: New → Confirmed → Preparing → Ready → Completed (or
  Cancelled). Change it from the order page; customize the stages themselves
  in **Settings → Order Stages**.
- **Payment status**: Unpaid → Pending Verification → Partially Paid → Paid
  → Refunded. **Opening a payment app (Venmo, CashApp, etc.) never marks an
  order paid** — you decide when to mark it paid, after you've actually
  checked your account. Use **Record Payment** / **Record Refund** on the
  order page.

From an order's page you can also: edit quantities or add products, apply a
discount or custom charge, add internal notes (only you see these) or a
customer-visible note, cancel (this restocks tracked inventory), duplicate
(handy for a repeat order), print a summary, and message the customer using
call/text/email buttons that open your iPhone's native apps.

## Customers

**Customers** shows everyone who's ordered, with total orders, total
revenue, and full order history. Duplicate customers are prevented
automatically by matching phone number and email. Add private notes
(allergies, preferences, VIP status) on their page.

## Messages

**Settings → Messages** has editable templates (order received, payment
reminder, ready for pickup, etc.) with placeholders like `{{first_name}}`
and `{{order_number}}` that fill in automatically. Every order and customer
page has a message composer — pick a template, edit if needed, then tap Call,
Text, or Email to open your phone's native app with everything pre-filled.

## Reports

**Reports** shows gross vs. collected revenue (only actual recorded payments
count as "collected"), outstanding balances, best sellers, revenue by
payment method, and repeat-customer rate. Export orders or customers as CSV
from Reports or the Customers page.

## Removing the demo products

If you loaded the sample seed data, go to **Products**, open each demo item
(marked "Demo"), and tap **Archive** — or delete permanently from the same
page.
